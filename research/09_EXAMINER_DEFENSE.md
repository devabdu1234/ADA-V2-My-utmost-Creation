# Examiner Defense: ADA-V2 Academic Shield

## Q1: "This is just an app. Where is the research?"
**Defense:** The research is not in the software, but in the **Interaction Model**. We are investigating the *cost of interruption*. The software is the experimental apparatus used to test a specific hypothesis about cognitive load and modality switching.

## Q2: "Weighted scoring isn't novel; it's basic math."
**Defense:** Correct. The scoring is not the novelty; the **application of that scoring to trigger a native S2S loop** is. We are moving from "Notification $\rightarrow$ User Attention" to "Perception $\rightarrow$ Decision $\rightarrow$ Seamless Audio Intervention."

## Q3: "How do you justify your weights (e.g., 3.0 for confidence)?"
**Defense:** These are **Empirical Heuristics**. They serve as a baseline for our experimental phase. Part of the research is using a "Grid Search" or "Ablation Study" to find the optimal weights that maximize utility while minimizing annoyance.

## Q4: "What happens if the AI misses a critical event because of a cooldown?"
**Defense:** We implement a **Safety Override**. Any event flagged as "Critical/Escalated" by the LLM bypasses the temporal cooldowns to ensure a 100% recall rate for high-consequence events.

## Q5: "How is this different from a smart-watch notification?"
**Defense:** A smart-watch is still a "Pull" mechanism (you must look at it). ADA-V2 is a "Push" mechanism that uses native audio-to-audio modalities to deliver information without requiring the user to shift their visual focus.

## Q6: "Is native S2S just an API call? Where is the novelty?"
**Defense:** The API provides the *capability*, but the research lies in the *application of that capability* to solve the 'Interruption Paradox'. We are not claiming to have invented S2S; we are claiming that S2S, when triggered by a semantic utility engine, reduces cognitive resumption lag in a way that cascaded TTS (Text-to-Speech) cannot. The novelty is the **integrated loop of Perception $\rightarrow$ Utility Scoring $\rightarrow$ Native Modality Delivery**.
