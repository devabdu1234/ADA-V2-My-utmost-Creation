# Research Direction: From Reactive Tool to Cognitive Load Manager

## 1. The Pivot
Following a rigorous forensic audit and novelty challenge, the research direction for ADA-V2 is pivoted from "A Proactive AI Assistant" to **"A Multimodal Framework for the Mitigation of Cognitive Resumption Lag in Proactive Human-AI Interaction."**

## 2. The Core Thesis
Traditional proactive notifications (visual/textual) impose a high "context-switch penalty," forcing users to disengage from their primary task to process a notification. ADA-V2 leverages **Native Speech-to-Speech (S2S) interaction** to deliver information through a secondary cognitive channel, hypothesized to reduce the "resumption lag" (the time required to return to a flow state) compared to visual interruptions.

## 3. The Research Problem
Existing proactive systems solve for *what* to notify (priority scoring) but fail to solve for *how* the notification impacts the user's current cognitive state. There is a lack of evidence on whether native, low-latency voice interventions are less disruptive than visual notifications for high-urgency, low-complexity updates.

## 4. Justification for Novelty
While the individual components (S2S, Priority Scoring, MCP) exist, the **integrated loop**—perceiving an external event $\rightarrow$ scoring its cognitive cost $\rightarrow$ triggering a native S2S intervention—represents a novel approach to "Seamless Agency."

## 5. Success Metric
The project is successful if it can empirically demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in **Task Resumption Time** and **NASA-TLX Cognitive Load scores** when using S2S proactivity versus standard OS notifications.
