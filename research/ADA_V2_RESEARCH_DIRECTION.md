# ADA-V2: Research Direction & Academic Framing

## 1. Comparison of Viable Research Directions

We have evaluated several potential research paths for the ADA-V2 project.

| Direction | Academic Defensibility | Feasibility | Research Gap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unified Architecture** | Low (Engineering focus) | High | Integration is not inherently novel. |
| **Tool Orchestration** | Medium (Technical focus) | Medium | Many "Agent" frameworks already do this. |
| **Proactive Logic** | Medium (Logic focus) | High | Scoring is established (NEVA/Horvitz). |
| **S2S Proactivity** | **High (HCI focus)** | **High** | **Native S2S as a modality to reduce cognitive load is a genuine gap.** |

## 2. Recommended Research Question
**"How does proactive conversational speech-to-speech interaction, compared with conventional notification approaches, affect task-resumption time, perceived cognitive workload, and critical-information retention during ongoing tasks?"**

### Why this is the strongest direction:
1. **Narrow Scope:** It focuses on one specific, measurable effect (Resumption Lag) rather than "making a general assistant."
2. **Measurable:** It uses objective timestamps (T1/T2) and validated instruments (NASA-TLX).
3. **High Novelty:** It leverages the native S2S loop as a *research tool* to test a hypothesis about human cognition, moving the project from "software engineering" to "HCI research."

## 3. Supporting Objectives
- **Objective 1:** Compare the "Resumption Lag" of S2S interruptions vs. Visual interruptions.
- **Objective 2:** Quantify the "Cognitive Load" using the NASA-TLX scale across modalities.
- **Objective 3:** Evaluate the "Recall Rate" of critical information to ensure proactivity doesn't sacrifice accuracy.

## 4. Academic Justification
The project will contribute to the field of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** by providing empirical evidence on whether a seamless audio-to-audio interaction reduces the "context-switch penalty" typically associated with proactive AI alerts.
